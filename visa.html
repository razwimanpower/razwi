Visit Visa

Assistance with visit visa enquiries.

Visa requirements depend on the destination and the applicant. Approval is decided only by the relevant authorities. We cannot guarantee approval.

General documents (varies by country)

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Travel itinerary or ticket enquiry details
  • Proof of funds or employment, if required

How visa assistance works

  1. Enquire
    Destination, nationality and purpose.
  2. Checklist
    We tell you the documents usually required.
  3. Application
    We help prepare and submit it.
  4. Decision
    The authorities decide. Timelines vary.

Questions

Will my visa be approved?

We cannot guarantee it. Only the relevant authorities decide.

How long does it take?

It depends on the country and applicant. We will share the current guidance.

Are visa fees refundable?

Government fees are generally not. See the refund policy.
