Planning your Umrah

  1. Share your plan
    Number of travellers, month and hotel preferences.
  2. Receive options
    We share package options for your review.
  3. Confirm in writing
    Inclusions, exclusions and charges are listed.
  4. Documents
    We guide you on documents required by the authorities.

Documents usually needed

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Any health or vaccination records required at the time
  • Identity and address proof

Requirements are set by the authorities and may change. Final details are always confirmed by our team in writing.

Questions

Can I travel with family?

Yes. Tell us the number of travellers and any special needs.
